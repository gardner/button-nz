## How to read it

- **The shares are scenarios, not forecasts.** Nothing here says how many owners would build.
- **People housed** runs from one person a flat to the town's own 2.1 people per household: a one-bedroom flat may house fewer than an average home.
- **The areas differ.** The census's Reefton (a statistical area) and the town screen's Residential zones aren't the same boundary, so the increase is indicative.
- **Rounding.** Stats NZ randomly rounds counts to protect confidentiality, so parts may not sum to totals.
- **It's a screen, not consent:** hazards, slope, the district plan and services aren't checked (see the town screen's report).
