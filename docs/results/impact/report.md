# Impact: what the town screen would mean for Reefton's housing

Code `10adb91`. Properties from the town screen ([report](../town-screen/report.md)), counted by title. Today's housing from Stats NZ, 2023 Census place summary, https://tools.summaries.stats.govt.nz/places/SA2/reefton (retrieved 2026-11-01, CC BY 4.0 (Stats NZ)): 603 private dwellings, 489 households and 1,026 people in 2023; households average 1.9 people, and 42% are one person.

Of Reefton's 959 residential properties, 745 have a building on them. Of those **backyards**, 439 have room for a real prefab on LINZ's building outlines (with 0.6 m eaves), and 235 also clear of everything over 2 m on the LiDAR. A flat there is a second home beside the first: what the exemption adds.

## How to read it

- **The shares are scenarios, not forecasts.** Nothing here says how many owners would build.
- **People housed** runs from one person a flat to Reefton's average household of 1.9 (from Stats NZ's households by size).
- **The areas differ.** The census's Reefton (a statistical area) and the town screen's Residential zones aren't the same boundary, so the increase is indicative.
- **Rounding.** Stats NZ randomly rounds counts to protect confidentiality, so parts may not sum to totals.
- **It's a screen, not consent:** hazards, slope, the district plan and services aren't checked (see the town screen's report).
