# Suppliers, professionals and utilities for 35 Victory Street, Reefton

A sourced list of the prefab products, professionals, trades and utilities a granny flat at
35 Victory Street, Reefton (Buller District, West Coast) would need. It assumes a new detached
dwelling of 70 m² or less under the granny-flat building consent exemption (Schedule 1A of the
Building Act 2004, in force from 15 January 2026).

Researched 2026-10-30. Every fact links to its source; nothing is estimated. Nothing here is an
endorsement or a price quote.
**"Unverified" means call before use**: it was seen only in a directory or search snippet. The
[LBP][lbp] and [CSLB][cslb] registers don't show employers, so firm-to-licence links are name
matches only. "Self-declared" means a membership not checked on its register. No individual is
named; mobiles and personal emails are left out even where published.

## 1. What the exemption requires

Sources: MBIE's [Exemption Guidance][guide] (Version 2, effective 8 October 2026), the
[designers][fs-design] and [building professionals][fs-build] fact sheets (January 2026), the
[Foundations Guidance][found] (August 2026) and Buller District Council's (BDC)
[granny flat page][bdc-gf]. **The building** must be new, standalone, single-storey and
self-contained; 70 m² or less including any internal garage; at least 2 m from other
residential buildings and legal boundaries, and not built across a boundary between allotments,
even on one title (Building Act s 42B; [guidance p.53][guide]); a steel or timber frame, or a
certified composite structural product (added by the 8 October 2026 amendment); roof cladding
no heavier than 20 kg/m² and wall cladding no heavier than 220 kg/m² ([BRANZ BU704][bu704]);
floor no more than 1 m above ground and height no more than 4 m above floor; no level-entry
shower needing a membrane; electric or gas heating only; full Building Code compliance with "no
waivers or modifications". Services must connect to networks where available, with an
**independent point of supply** for electricity (and gas, if used). Drainage is limited to 30
fixture units, no pumped systems, a DN100 main drain at 1:60 and DN65 branches at 1:40. For a
unit built off-site, "a project information memorandum (PIM) is only required for the final
location where the building will be placed" ([guidance p.22][guide]).

### Open questions

1. **Hazards.** The [Foundations Guidance][found] says a site the PIM marks "likely"
   hazard-affected is "not suitable" for the exemption unless council confirms adequate
   provision; the [Exemption Guidance][guide] calls PIM comments "advisory only" if section 71(2)
   mitigation is documented. Reefton sits beside the Inangahua River.
2. **Two habitable buildings.** [BAM 422][bam422] says the Buller District Plan limits habitable
   buildings in Residential and Commercial zones "to a maximum of two per site, even if they are
   not self-contained". [BDC][bdc-gf] warns resource consent may still be needed. Whether that
   plan or the [Te Tai o Poutini Plan][ttpp] governs wasn't researched. Ask the Duty Planner.
3. **Stormwater.** The [LTP 2025–2034][ltp] says Reefton's piped wastewater "also convey
   stormwater" (p.113) and that "Reefton does not have an existing stormwater network" (p.58).
   Ask BDC whether to use a kerb outlet, open drain or soakage.
4. **Contributions.** BDC has only RMA financial contributions, no development contributions
   policy ([LTP pp.231–233][ltp]), and no published granny-flat amount. MfE's
   [NES-DMRU FAQ][nes-faq] says councils can still charge development contributions. The Te Tai o
   Poutini Plan chapter is [under appeal][ttpp-appeals], and its [recommended text][ttpp-fc]
   charges reserves "at the time of building consent". How BDC applies either is unknown.
5. **Fees.** The 2026/27 "Granny flats" block ([BAM 455][bam455]; [fees p.58][fees]) lists a
   $3,000 deposit, PIM $235 plus processing, a $400 levy, a $105 planning check, and consent,
   inspection and CCC fees, though BDC doesn't inspect or certify exempt flats. Confirm which
   apply. Online lodging is through Objective Build ([building page][bdc-bld]).
6. **Coast Waters.** [Coast Waters Ltd][coastwaters] takes over water, wastewater and stormwater
   "from mid-2027" and has published no connection process yet.

## 3. Professionals

No designer, surveyor or structural engineer with a website was found in Reefton; Westport and
Greymouth, each about 80 km away, are the nearest hubs. Ground outside MBIE's "good" or "suitable"
limits needs specific engineering design ([Foundations][found]); that is one reason to need an
engineer, not the only one. The designer confirms whether the chosen building, such as a
proprietary steel-base or solid-timber system, needs specific engineering. None of the geotechnical
firms names the Alpine Fault.

### Electricity (Westpower)

Westpower runs the network "across Westland, Grey, and part of the Buller Districts"
([about](https://westpower.co.nz/about)); [Wikipedia][wiki-wp] names Reefton as a network town.
The independent point of supply means a new connection (ICP), not a sub-circuit off the house
(a reading of [guidance p.33][guide]). The steps ([how to get connected][wp-connect]):

1. The electrical contractor submits a [Customer Initiated Work Request][ciwr] (7 working days).
2. Westpower issues a Network Connection Agreement with a new ICP, valid 6 months; the owner's
   retailer accepts the ICP.
3. After wiring, the contractor sends the agreement, Certificate of Compliance, Record of
   Inspection and a requested date, with at least 48 hours' notice ([contractors'
   procedure][wp-contractors]), to connections@westpower.co.nz. Only a Westpower Approved
   Contractor connects and energises; it confirms the date, which "may be different" from the
   one requested.

From 1 April 2026: **$500 ex GST** connection charge, plus $339/kVA at the distribution-substation
tier ($0 at others), plus contractor and retailer costs ([charges][wp-charges]). No boundary
connection point means a larger job or a [network extension][wp-ext]. Phone 03 768 9300.

### Telecoms (Chorus)

- Reefton is a completed UFB town ([CIP][cip]). [Broadband Map NZ](https://broadbandmap.nz/)
  shows Chorus fibre, fixed wireless and Gravity satellite on Victory Street (street level only).
- Chorus requires "an individual 20mm green lead-in pipe" per house, not shared with power, at
  least 450 mm deep ([lead-in guide][chorus-leadin]). Call before you dig: 0800 248 344.
- Pre-built fibre needs a council-advised address ([guide][chorus-prebuilt]); how BDC addresses
  a second dwelling wasn't found. Search summaries (unverified) say a standard install starts
  with a retailer order and is free up to 200 m from the boundary.
